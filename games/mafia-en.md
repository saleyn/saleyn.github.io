**Number of Players:** 5–16 players + 1 Moderator

**Key Feature:** Eliminated players remain in the game, have a limited right to speak during daytime discussions (including the ability to reveal checks and bluff), and form the **Council of Shadows**, voting simultaneously with living players to award a collective **+1 Shadow Vote**.

---

## 1. Roles and Team Composition (5–16 Players)

### Role Distribution

| Total Players | Mafia + Don | Town (including Commissaire and Doctor) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **5** | **1** (1 Mafia) | **All others** (1 Commissaire, no Doctor) |
| **6–7** | **2** (1 Mafia + 1 Don) | **All others** (1 Commissaire, no Doctor) |
| **8–9** | **2** (1 Mafia + 1 Don) | **All others** (1 Commissaire + 1 Doctor) |
| **10–12** | **3** (2 Mafia + 1 Don) | **All others** (1 Commissaire + 1 Doctor) |
| **13–16** | **4** (3 Mafia + 1 Don) | **All others** (1 Commissaire + 1 Doctor) |

### Role Descriptions

**Mafia Team (Black Cards)**

- **Don:** Head of the Mafia (included in games of 6 or more players). Wakes up at night with the Mafia to choose a victim, and then wakes up separately to check one player per night to find the **Commissaire**. Holds the deciding vote if the Mafia cannot agree on a target.
- **Mafia:** Wake up together at night to agree on eliminating one player.

**Town Team (Red Cards)**

- **Commissaire (always 1):** Wakes up every night and checks one player. The Moderator silently indicates whether that player is **Mafia** (thumbs down / nod) or **Town / Citizen** (thumbs up / head shake). *Note: To the Commissaire, the Don registers as regular Mafia.*
- **Doctor (1 in games of 8 or more players):** Wakes up every night and chooses one player to save from the night kill. Cannot heal the same player two nights in a row (may heal themselves once per game, or as agreed upon by the table).
- **Citizens (all remaining players):** Have no night abilities. Hunt the Mafia using logic, observation, and daytime voting.

## 2. Game Phases

### Phase 1: Night

All players — **both living and dead** — close their eyes and maintain complete silence. *(Tip: Play background music or lightly tap on the table to mask accidental noises).*

1. **Mafia Wakes Up:** Living Mafia players (including the Don, if in play) silently agree on eliminating one living player. The Mafia goes back to sleep.
2. **Don's Check (6+ players):** The Don wakes up alone and points to one living player. The Moderator nods **"Yes"** if the target is the Commissaire, or shakes their head **"No"** for any other role. The Don goes back to sleep.
3. **Commissaire Wakes Up:** The Commissaire points to one living player. The Moderator indicates **Mafia** or **Citizen**. The Commissaire goes back to sleep.
4. **Doctor Wakes Up (8+ players):** The Doctor points to one living player to save them. The Moderator notes the choice, and the Doctor goes back to sleep.

### Phase 2: Morning and Day

1. **Morning Announcement:** Everyone opens their eyes. The Moderator announces who was killed during the night (or states that the Doctor saved the victim).
2. **Closed Roles Rule:** **Roles and team alignments are NEVER revealed by the Moderator after death** (neither upon a night kill nor after a daytime elimination).
3. **Daytime Speaking Order & Limits:**
   - **Step A — Living Players' Turns (30–45 seconds each):**
     - **Rotating Start:** On Day 1, speaking starts with Player #1 and proceeds clockwise. On each subsequent day, the starting turn shifts clockwise by one living seat (e.g., Day 2 starts with Player #2, Day 3 with Player #3, skipping eliminated seats) so no player is stuck speaking first or last every round.
     - **Strict Monologue:** Only the active speaker may talk. Interrupting, whispering, or reacting aloud during another player's turn is prohibited.
     - **Nominations (Final & Irreversible):** During their own turn, a living player may nominate **at most one** living suspect for voting (*"I nominate Player #... for voting"*). A player already nominated that day cannot be nominated again. **Nominations are final and cannot be revoked** once the speaker's turn ends (an accidental slip of the tongue may only be corrected immediately before the speaker's timer expires).
   - **Step B — Word of the Council of Shadows (20–30 seconds per dead player):**
     - Immediately after all living players finish their turns, each eliminated player receives **one brief monologue (20–30 seconds)** in order of elimination (or seat order).
     - During this time, a dead player may freely share theories, claim their role, announce night-check results, or bluff.
     - Dead players **cannot** nominate candidates, and living players may not interrupt them.
   - **Step C — Open Discussion (1–2 minutes):**
     - Takes place **strictly among living players** right before voting.
     - **No new nominations** may be made during open discussion, and existing nominations remain locked in.
     - Dead players must remain completely silent during open discussion so as not to interrupt the table and to let the living decide for themselves which ghosts to trust.

## 3. Afterlife Rules: The Council of Shadows

When a player is killed at night or eliminated during the day:

- **No Night Actions:** Dead players keep their eyes closed at night, do not wake up with their team, and lose all night abilities (even if they were the Don, Commissaire, or Doctor).
- **Limited Daytime Speaking Right:** Dead players get exactly **20–30 seconds** once per day during Step B to speak, reveal checks, or bluff, after which they must remain silent until the next day (except for their voting gesture).
- **Immunity:** Dead players cannot be nominated, voted out, or killed at night.
- **Joining the Council of Shadows:** All eliminated players immediately become part of the **Council of Shadows**.

---

## 4. Voting Phase (Single-Shot Finger Voting)

If only one player is nominated on the first day, no vote is held (standard club rule — or held at the table's discretion). When multiple suspects are nominated, voting takes place in **a single unified round**:

1. **Assigning Numbers to Nominees:** The Moderator assigns sequential numbers **1, 2, 3...** to the nominated candidates in the order they were nominated and announces the list aloud:
*"Nominee #1 is Player #5, Nominee #2 is Player #8, Nominee #3 is Player #2. Everyone votes simultaneously! One, two, three!"*
2. **Simultaneous Gesture (Strictly on the count of "Three"):**
- **Living Players (mandatory vote for 1 nominee):** Place **1, 2, 3... fingers** of one hand on the table (if there are more than 5 nominees, use both hands) corresponding to the number of their chosen candidate. Every living player **must** vote for strictly one nominee — it is physically impossible to vote for two, and any player with no fingers on the table is immediately visible to the Moderator.
- **Council of Shadows (Dead Players):** Raise **1, 2, 3... fingers** in the air corresponding to their chosen candidate's number, or show a **closed fist** if they wish to abstain.
- *Fingers shown or changed after the count of "three" are not counted.*

3. **Counting the +1 Shadow Vote:**
- **Minimum Quorum:** The Council of Shadows activates once there are **at least 2 dead players** in the game (so the very first victim does not receive a solo bonus vote).
- **Strict Majority:** If a nominee receives a **strict majority** of votes from all dead players (for example, 3 out of 4 ghosts or 4 out of 6 ghosts), that nominee receives **+1 additional vote** added to their living player votes.
- If the shadow votes are tied or no nominee achieves a strict majority, **no bonus vote is awarded**.

4. **Eliminating a Player:** The nominated player with the highest total vote count (**Living Votes + Shadow Vote**) is eliminated.
- *In case of a tie:* Tied nominees receive **30 seconds** each for a defense speech, followed by a revote on fingers between those tied candidates only (#1 and #2). If the tie persists, the table votes on whether to keep or eliminate both (or resolves it per prior agreement with the Moderator).

---

## 5. Victory Conditions

Victory conditions are determined solely by **living players**:

- **Town Victory:** All living Mafia players (including the Don) are eliminated.
- **Mafia Victory:** The number of living Mafia players equals or exceeds the number of living Town players (for example, 2 Mafia vs. 2 Citizens, or 1 Mafia vs. 1 Citizen).

---

## 6. Moderator Cheat Sheet

- **Before the Game Starts:** Remind players that roles are not revealed upon death, the dead sleep at night with their eyes closed, daytime speaking follows strict monologues (no interruptions), nominations are **final**, each ghost gets **only one 20–30 second speech** after the living speak (and stays silent during open discussion), and voting happens on a **single count of "three"** (**fingers on the table** matching the nominee's number for living players; **fingers in the air** or a closed fist for the Council of Shadows).
- **At Night:** Ensure dead players keep their eyes closed — especially eliminated Mafia members, so they do not see upcoming night targets.
- **During the Day:** Rotate the starting living speaker clockwise each day, record nominations in order (do not allow revocations once a turn ends), strictly enforce the ghosts' 20–30 second timer, and cut off any remarks from dead players during living players' speeches and open discussion.
- **During Voting:** Number the nominees (#1, #2, #3...), count *"One, two, three!"*, verify that every living player has a hand on the table showing their chosen number of fingers, tally the living votes for each number, and then check whether any nominee achieved a strict majority of raised fingers from the Council of Shadows to award the **+1 Shadow Vote**.
