**Player Count:** 5–16 Players + 1 Moderator

**Core Mechanic:** Eliminated players remain in the game, participate in daytime discussions, and form the **Council of Shadows**, voting simultaneously with the living to award a collective **+1 Shadow Vote**.

---

## 1. Roles & Team Setup (5–16 Players)

### Role Distribution

| Total Players | Mafia + Don | Town (including Commissar & Doctor) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **5** | **1** (1 Mafia) | **All remaining** (1 Commissar, no Doctor) |
| **6–7** | **2** (1 Mafia + 1 Don) | **All remaining** (1 Commissar, no Doctor) |
| **8–9** | **2** (1 Mafia + 1 Don) | **All remaining** (1 Commissar + 1 Doctor) |
| **10–12** | **3** (2 Mafia + 1 Don) | **All remaining** (1 Commissar + 1 Doctor) |
| **13–16** | **4** (3 Mafia + 1 Don) | **All remaining** (1 Commissar + 1 Doctor) |

### Role Descriptions

**Mafia Team (Black Cards)**

* **The Don:** Leader of the Mafia (included in games of 6+ players). Wakes up at night with the Mafia to choose a victim, then wakes up alone to check one player per night to identify the **Commissar**. Has the final say if the Mafia cannot agree on a target.
* **Mafia:** Wake up together at night to agree on eliminating one player.

**Town Team (Red Cards)**

* **The Commissar (always 1):** Wakes up each night to investigate one player. The Moderator silently signals whether that player is **Mafia** (thumbs-down / nod) or **Town** (thumbs-up / head shake). *Note: The Don registers as regular Mafia to the Commissar.*
* **The Doctor (1 in games of 8+ players):** Wakes up each night to choose one player to save from being killed that night. Cannot protect the same player two nights in a row (may self-protect once per game, or as agreed by the table).
* **Citizens (all remaining players):** Have no night abilities. Rely on logic, observation, and daytime voting to find the Mafia.

## 2. Game Phases

### Phase 1: Night

All players—**both living and dead**—close their eyes and remain completely silent. *(Tip: Play background music or lightly tap the table to mask subtle noises).*

1. **Mafia Wakes Up:** Living Mafia members (including the Don, if in play) silently agree on one living player to eliminate. Mafia goes back to sleep.
2. **Don's Check (6+ players):** The Don wakes up alone and points to one living player. The Moderator nods **Yes** if that player is the Commissar, or shakes **No** for any other role. The Don goes back to sleep.
3. **Commissar Wakes Up:** The Commissar points to one living player. The Moderator signals **Mafia** or **Town**. The Commissar goes back to sleep.
4. **Doctor Wakes Up (8+ players):** The Doctor points to one living player to save. The Moderator notes the choice, and the Doctor goes back to sleep.

### Phase 2: Morning & Day

1. **Morning Announcement:** Everyone opens their eyes. The Moderator announces who was killed during the night (or announces that the Doctor saved the target).
2. **Hidden Roles Rule:** **Roles and team alignments are NEVER revealed upon death** (neither after a night kill nor after a daytime execution).
3. **Discussion & Nominations:**
* **Both living and dead players** participate freely in the discussion, sharing theories, accusing suspects, and bluffing.
* Give each player **30–45 seconds** of speaking time in turn order (followed by a 1–2 minute open debate) so large tables keep moving smoothly.
* During their turn, living players may **nominate** a living suspect for voting (*"I nominate Player #..."*). Voting only takes place among nominated candidates.



---

## 3. Rules of the Afterlife: Council of Shadows

When a player is killed at night or executed during the day:

* **No Night Actions:** Dead players keep their eyes closed at night, do not wake up with their team, and lose all night abilities (even if they were the Don, Commissar, or Doctor).
* **Full Daytime Voice:** Dead players may speak, argue, and bluff during every Day phase.
* **Immunity:** Dead players cannot be nominated, executed, or killed at night.
* **Joining the Council of Shadows:** All eliminated players immediately become members of the **Council of Shadows**.

---

## 4. Voting Phase (Simultaneous Vote)

If only one player is nominated on Day 1, no vote takes place (standard club rule—or hold the vote if preferred by the table). When multiple suspects are nominated:

1. **Calling the Vote:** The Moderator calls each nominated player one by one in the order they were nominated:
*"Who votes to eliminate Player #5? One, two, three!"*
2. **Simultaneous Gesture (Strictly on "Three"):**
* **Living Players:** Raise an **open hand** to vote for elimination.
* **Council of Shadows (Dead Players):** Give a **thumbs-up** to cast their council vote.
* *Hands or gestures raised after the count of three do not count.*


3. **One Vote Per Player:** Each living player and each dead player may vote for **only one** candidate per round. Any player (living or dead) who has not voted by the final candidate automatically casts their vote on the last nominee.
4. **Calculating the +1 Shadow Vote:**
* **Minimum Quorum:** The Council of Shadows activates once **at least 2 players** are dead (so the very first casualty does not get a solo bonus vote).
* **Strict Majority:** If a candidate receives a **strict majority** of all dead players' votes (e.g., 3 out of 4 ghosts, or 4 out of 6 ghosts), that candidate receives **+1 bonus vote** added to their living vote total.
* If the ghosts split their votes evenly or no candidate achieves a strict majority, **no bonus vote** is awarded.


5. **Elimination:** The nominated player with the highest combined total (**Living Votes + Shadow Vote**) is eliminated.
* *In case of a tie:* Tied candidates get **30 seconds** each for a defense speech, followed by a revote between only the tied players. If still tied, the table votes to spare or eliminate both (or follows the Moderator's pre-game ruling).


---

## 5. Win Conditions

Win conditions are counted strictly by **living players**:

* **Town Wins:** All living Mafia members (including the Don) are eliminated.
* **Mafia Wins:** The number of living Mafia members equals or outnumbers the number of living Town players (e.g., 2 Mafia vs. 2 Town, or 1 Mafia vs. 1 Town).

---

## 6. Moderator Quick-Reference Checklist

* **Before the Game:** Remind players that roles remain hidden upon death, dead players sleep with their eyes closed at night, and voting happens strictly on the count of three (open hand for living, thumbs-up for the Council of Shadows).
* **At Night:** Watch to ensure dead players keep their eyes closed—especially eliminated Mafia members, so they do not learn future night targets.
* **During Voting:** Count living open hands first, then check if the Council of Shadows reached a strict majority of thumbs-up to award the **+1 Shadow Vote**.
